Emergency sewer line repair in Grapevine when cleaning no longer holds

Your sewer line carries every drain in the house to the city main. Sewer line repair in Grapevine fixes a section of that line that has cracked, shifted, collapsed or filled with roots. The 24/7 line at (817) 601-6266 takes calls any hour, and a plumber books the soonest appointment available. Same-day appointments are offered when the schedule allows.

If sewage is backing up into the tub, shower or a floor drain, stop using water in the house at once. Do not run the dishwasher or washer, and keep children and pets away from the affected area. A sewage backup is a health hazard and a damage risk.

Not every backup is a broken pipe. Many are clogs. The way to tell is a camera, and that is how a repair is chosen.

Sewer backup: what to do first

A backup that comes up through the lowest drain is a sign the main line is blocked or broken. Do this:

  • Stop all water use in the house. Do not flush.
  • Keep people and pets out of the wet area and avoid contact with the water.
  • Do not use chemical drain cleaners in a backed-up line.
  • Call the 24/7 line and tell the dispatcher it is a sewage backup.
  • If neighbors have the same problem, the blockage may be in the city main, so call the City of Grapevine at (817) 410-3000.

Signs the sewer line has failed

A clog usually clears with cleaning. A failed pipe keeps coming back.

Repeat backups

A line that clogs again within weeks of cleaning has a cause such as roots, a belly or a collapse.

Gurgling and slow drains throughout

Several fixtures slow together and the toilet gurgles when the washer drains.

A sewage smell in the yard or house

Gas escapes from a cracked pipe. A green patch of lawn over the line can also point to a leak.

A sunken area in the yard

Soil that washes into a broken pipe leaves a dip over the line.

Cast iron and older pipe

Older homes may have cast iron drain pipe under the slab or in the walls. Cast iron lasts for decades, but the inside can scale and the pipe can crack or corrode through. A camera shows the interior and the condition of the joints.

A cracked cast iron pipe can sometimes be repaired with a sleeve at one point or lined if the pipe is otherwise sound. If the pipe is crumbling throughout, replacement is the honest answer. We do not claim that Grapevine's homes have any particular pipe, because we have not found a primary source for the local housing stock. A camera on your own line tells you what you have.

The same camera check is the heart of a sewer inspection, and it should come before any repair is quoted.

Permits and the city's side of the line

The City of Grapevine publishes a combined electrical, plumbing, mechanical and irrigation permit application, and its Building Services office at (817) 410-3165 can tell you whether a repair needs a permit and an inspection. Where the line runs under a driveway, a sidewalk or a public right-of-way, ask the city before digging.

Ask the city, and your plumber, which part of the line is yours. A plumber who checks first avoids repairing the wrong section.

Questions about sewer line repair in Grapevine

Do you handle sewer line emergencies at night or on weekends in Grapevine?

Yes. The line at (817) 601-6266 is open 24/7. A sewage backup is treated as an emergency, and the dispatcher books the soonest appointment available.

How do I know if my sewer line needs repair, not just cleaning?

A camera shows it. If the same line backs up again after cleaning, or the camera finds cracks, offsets or roots in the pipe wall, repair is the answer.

What is trenchless sewer repair?

It repairs or replaces the pipe with little digging. Lining and pipe bursting are the two main methods. Not every pipe suits them, and the camera decides.

How long does sewer line repair take?

A spot repair can be done in a day. Lining or a full replacement takes longer, and the plumber gives you the time with the price.

Does my insurance cover sewer line repair?

Rarely for the pipe itself. A sudden backup that damages the house may be covered by an endorsement. Read your policy or ask your agent.

A failed sewer line gets worse over time. Roots grow, cracks widen and a belly collects more waste. The sooner a camera shows the problem, the more repair options you have.

Who we are

Grapevine Express Plumbing is a 24/7 plumbing line for Grapevine, Texas. You call (817) 601-6266, a dispatcher listens to the problem and the licensed, insured plumber who takes the job finds the cause, gives you a clear price before work starts and does the repair if you approve it.

We keep the business simple on purpose. The website explains plumbing problems the way a good plumber would explain them at your kitchen table, so you can decide what is urgent and what can wait until morning. The phone line does the rest.

How a job works

Every job follows the same order. The plumber looks at the problem first, then tells you what is wrong, what the repair options are and what each costs. Nothing starts until you approve the price. If a repair is not worth doing, for example a water heater with a rusted tank, the plumber says so and shows you the replacement option instead.

Appointments are same-day when the schedule allows. We do not promise an arrival time, because traffic on State Highway 114 and State Highway 121 and the job before yours both move it.

Licensing, and how to check it yourself

Plumbers in Texas are regulated by the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners. The board's Public License Search lets you look up any plumber or plumbing company by name or license, and you should do that for any contractor before work begins. We send licensed and insured plumbers, and if you want to confirm the license of the plumber on your job, ask for it on arrival.

On permits, the City of Grapevine publishes a combined electrical, plumbing, mechanical and irrigation permit application, and lists the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) as its plumbing code, and its Building Services office at (817) 410-3165 can tell you which permit your job needs.

How pricing works

Plumbing prices depend on what is wrong and what is behind the wall, so a number given blind over the phone is a guess. We do not guess. The plumber looks, explains the cause and gives you a clear price before work starts. If you decline, you decide what happens next.

Each service page on this site lists what drives the price for that job, such as access, parts, the time of day and any permit. That is so you can compare any plumber's quote with the real cost factors, not only ours.

How to tell an emergency from a job that can wait

Call right away for sewage backing up, a burst pipe, a leak you cannot stop, no water at all or any gas smell. For a gas smell, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility from outside first. A slow drain, a dripping faucet or a running toilet can usually wait for the next available appointment, though they are worth fixing soon.

If you are not sure, call and describe it. A dispatcher can tell you whether it needs a plumber tonight, and you can shut off the water in the meantime.

How the guides on this site are written

The guides and service pages are written for homeowners who want to understand a problem before they pay for it. Local facts come from published city pages, the City of Grapevine's water quality report, the EPA and the Texas licensing board, and we leave a fact out when we cannot find it in a primary source.

Our blog covers how-to topics, such as replacing a toilet wax ring or sizing a softener. The blog is separate from the service pages, so a how-to guide is never a sales page in disguise.

What we do not do

We are a plumbing company. We do not do roofing, electrical work or mold remediation, and we do not pump septic tanks. If water damage has already soaked drywall or flooring, a restoration company handles drying and rebuild, and your plumber handles the source of the water. Your insurer will want both documented.

We also do not use stock testimonials or invented review counts on this site. If you want to judge us, call and ask questions, then check any contractor's license and insurance the way you would for anyone working in your home.

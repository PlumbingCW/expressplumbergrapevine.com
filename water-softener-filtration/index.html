What Grapevine's water report says

The City of Grapevine's 2024 water quality report shows hardness of 117 ppm for the city's own water and 135 ppm for water bought from the Trinity River Authority, measured as calcium carbonate. That is roughly 6.8 and 7.9 grains per gallon.

The USGS classes water as soft up to 60 mg/L, moderately hard from 61 to 120, hard from 121 to 180 and very hard above 180. Under that scale, 117 is at the top of moderately hard and 135 is hard. Which water reaches your tap depends on the supply mix, so a test at your own tap is the real answer.

The city's disinfectant is chloramine. A softener does not remove chloramine, and a carbon filter is the usual tool for taste and odor. For water quality questions, the city lists 817.410.3330.

What hard water does in a house

USGS notes that scale can shorten the life of equipment, raise heating costs and narrow pipes.

Scale on fixtures

White crust on faucets and showerheads, and spots on glass.

Water heater

Minerals settle in the tank and on elements. See water heater repair.

Appliances

Dishwashers and washing machines collect scale inside.

Soap and skin

Soap lathers less, and a film can remain.

Pipes

Scale builds up inside and can restrict flow over time.

Softener, filter or both

The two solve different problems.

Drains, power and salt

A softener needs a drain for its regeneration water, a nearby outlet and a place to add salt. If the house has no drain close to the main, the plumber plans the routing. The system should include a bypass valve so you can isolate it for service.

Some people limit sodium in their diet. A softener adds a small amount, and potassium salt is an option. Ask your doctor if this matters for you. A separate drinking water tap can supply untreated cold water.

Questions about water softener and filtration in Grapevine

Is Grapevine water hard?

The city's 2024 report lists 117 ppm for its own water and 135 ppm for Trinity River Authority water. On the USGS scale that is moderately hard to hard.

Will a softener remove chloramine?

No. A carbon filter is the usual way to reduce chlorine and chloramine taste and odor.

Do I need a softener or just a filter?

A softener treats hardness and a filter treats taste, odor or sediment. Many homes use both.

Can a softener help my water heater?

USGS notes that scale shortens equipment life and raises heating costs, so reducing hardness can help. Results vary by house.

Do I need a permit?

Possibly. Ask Grapevine Building Services at (817) 410-3165 before the work starts.

Treatment works best when it matches the water. Start with a test, size the system to the house and put in a bypass so it is easy to service.

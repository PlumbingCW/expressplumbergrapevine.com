Emergency sewer line cleaning in Grapevine for a blocked main line

The main sewer line is the single pipe that carries all of a house's wastewater to the city connection. Sewer line cleaning in Grapevine clears that pipe when it blocks and sewage backs up into the lowest drain. The 24/7 line at (817) 601-6266 takes calls at night and on weekends, and a plumber books the soonest appointment available. Same-day appointments are offered when the schedule allows.

Stop using water in the house. Each flush or load of laundry adds water that has nowhere to go. If the tub or a floor drain is already filling, keep people and pets away and avoid contact with the water.

Cleaning opens the line. It does not always explain why it blocked. This page covers how the line is cleaned and what to do about the cause.

Main line

When a main sewer line blockage is an emergency

A main line blockage is a different problem from a slow sink. Treat these signs as urgent:

  • Sewage coming up in a tub, shower or floor drain after a toilet flush or laundry.
  • Multiple fixtures draining slowly at once.
  • Gurgling from toilets and drains when other fixtures run.
  • A strong sewage smell in the house or yard.
  • A cleanout cap in the yard with standing water or sewage at it.

How a main sewer line is cleaned

The plumber works from the cleanout when there is one, and clears the line in stages.

1

Locate the cleanout

A cleanout is a capped pipe outside the house that gives direct access to the main line.

2

Cable the line

A drum machine with a cutting head clears the blockage.

3

Flush and verify

Water is run to confirm the line flows freely.

4

Camera

A camera pass shows what caused the blockage.

Common causes of main line blockages

Knowing the cause tells you whether cleaning will hold.

Tree roots

Roots enter at joints and grow into a mass. Cabling clears them, and the roots grow back until the joint is repaired.

Grease and wipes

Cooking grease hardens in the pipe. Wipes sold as flushable do not break down like paper.

A belly in the pipe

A low spot holds water and solids and clogs repeatedly.

A broken or collapsed section

Soil and roots enter and block the pipe. Cleaning does not hold.

Septic is a different system

Some homes in the wider area use septic tanks and not the city sewer. This page covers the sewer line between a house and the city connection. We do not list septic tank pumping or drain field work on this site, because it is a different trade.

If you are not sure which system your home uses, the water bill is a clue. A city sewer charge on the bill means the home is on the city system. If the question is still open, ask the City of Grapevine at (817) 410-3000.

Storm drains and yard drains

Storm drains are a separate system from the sanitary sewer. Water from roofs and yards drains to a storm system, and a clogged yard drain or area drain is a surface water problem, not a sewage backup. Clearing one is a simple job with a cable or a jet, and we can clear the drains on your property.

The city's storm drain inlets in the street are the city's responsibility. If one near your house is blocked and flooding the street, report it to the City of Grapevine at (817) 410-3000. A line from a downspout or a French drain that has clogged can be cleared with the same tools used on sewer lines, and the camera shows where the problem is.

Questions about sewer line cleaning in Grapevine

Do you handle sewer backup emergencies at night or on weekends in Grapevine?

Yes. The line at (817) 601-6266 is open 24/7. A sewage backup is treated as an emergency, and the dispatcher books the soonest appointment available.

How do I know whether it is the main sewer line or a branch?

If more than one fixture is slow or the lowest drain backs up when other fixtures run, it is probably the main line. If only one fixture is slow, it is the branch behind it.

Can I clear a main sewer line myself?

Rental machines exist, but cutting heads can damage pipe and the job is messy and risky. A camera pass afterward is part of doing it right.

Why does my sewer line keep clogging?

Roots, grease, a belly or a damaged pipe are the usual causes. A camera finds which one. A repeat blockage within a few weeks calls for a camera inspection.

Do you clear septic systems?

No. We cover the city sewer connection, not septic tank pumping or drain fields.

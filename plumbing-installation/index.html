Plumbing installation in Grapevine for remodels, new fixtures and added lines

Plumbing installation in Grapevine covers any new plumbing that is added to a house: new fixtures, relocated supply and drain lines, a new bathroom or laundry room, and gas piping for appliances. The 24/7 line at (817) 601-6266 also takes planning calls, and same-day appointments are offered when the schedule allows.

Installation differs from repair because it involves design choices and code compliance. The drain slope, the vent size, the supply routing and the permit all have to be correct. A plumber who plans the work first avoids opening finished walls again.

This page covers what a typical installation involves and what to ask before the work starts.

Permits, code and the city

The City of Grapevine lists the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), effective January 1, 2024, along with its own plumbing amendment. Its combined electrical, plumbing, mechanical and irrigation permit application is how the city handles plumbing permits, and Building Services at (817) 410-3165 can say which one your project needs.

A permitted job is inspected, and the inspection record is useful when you sell the house. If you are hiring a plumber, check the license on the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners Public License Search, and ask who is pulling the permit.

Gas lines: safety first

Gas piping for a range, a dryer, a water heater, a fireplace or an outdoor grill must be sized for the load and tested for leaks. Do not attempt gas work yourself.

If you smell gas, leave the building immediately and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Do not turn on lights, use the phone inside or try to find the source. Call a plumber only after the utility says it is safe. We do not promise a response time for a gas emergency.

Questions about plumbing installation in Grapevine

Do I need a permit for new plumbing in Grapevine?

Often yes, for anything beyond a like-for-like swap. Ask Grapevine Building Services at (817) 410-3165.

Can you move a toilet or a sink?

Usually. Drain slope and vent location decide how far, and the plumber checks before quoting.

Can you install a gas line for a grill or a dryer?

Yes, with a pressure test. A gas smell is an emergency, and you should call 911 or the gas utility first.

Do you do emergency installations?

Emergencies are usually repairs. If something has failed, the 24/7 line at (817) 601-6266 takes the call.

Can you work alongside my contractor?

Yes. Plumbing rough-in has to be coordinated with framing and inspections, and we work with general contractors.

Good plumbing installation is hidden: pipes that slope right, vents that work, valves you can reach. The test is a house that never needs the walls opened again.

Plumber service area around Grapevine, TX

Our dispatch address is 302 S Park Blvd, Grapevine, TX 76051, and from there we serve Grapevine ZIP codes 76051 and 76099 and take emergency calls across Tarrant County's north side. The emergency line at (817) 601-6266 is open 24/7.

Grapevine itself stretches from the Lake Grapevine shoreline and Rockledge Park in the north, through Historic Main Street and the Cotton Belt depot downtown, down to Grapevine Mills near State Highway 121 and the DFW Airport side of town. A call from any of those areas gets the same 24/7 dispatch and the same clear price before work starts.

Southlake, ZIP 76092

Southlake gets its own page because it is a separate city with its own water utility, its own building inspections office and its own after-hours public works line. Our emergency plumber page for Southlake covers those local details, including how Southlake's leak-adjustment rule for water bills works.

Southlake's ZIP code, 76092, is a nearby ZIP and not part of our core Grapevine coverage list, but the 24/7 line takes calls there too.

Other nearby towns

We also respond to emergency calls in Colleyville, Coppell, Euless, Flower Mound and Bedford. These places do not have separate pages here, because the services and the price process are the same and the page would only repeat what you can already read on our plumbing services page. Give the dispatcher the street address when you call and the soonest appointment available is booked.

If you are not sure whether your address is inside our service area, call and ask. A dispatcher can confirm it in a few seconds.

When you call from one of these towns, mention the cross streets or a nearby landmark as well as the address. It helps the plumber find the house quickly, especially on newer streets that a map app may not show correctly yet.

City offices that matter for plumbing

Plumbing problems sometimes involve the city as well as a plumber. A leak on the street side of the meter belongs to the city, and a permit for larger work is issued by the city where the house stands. These are the published numbers for the two cities with pages on this site.

Before any plumbing job, you can look up a plumber's license on the Public License Search at the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners. That is the state's own tool, and it works for every town on this page.

The City of Grapevine lists the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), effective January 1, 2024, with a plumbing amendment. Whether your job needs a permit depends on the work, so ask the building office for your city before you start.

How we decide where to list a town

A town gets its own page here only when there is something local to say that is true and checkable, such as its own permit office, its own water rules or its own utility numbers. Southlake meets that test. The smaller list of nearby towns does not, so those are named but not given pages of their own.

That keeps this site honest. A page for every nearby town would repeat the same paragraphs with a different name at the top, and that helps no one who is standing in a wet kitchen.

What to expect on a call

The dispatcher asks four things: what is happening, whether water is still running, the address and the best number to reach you. If water is running, you will be walked through shutting off the main valve first. Your appointment is booked for the soonest window available, and the plumber confirms the problem on site before quoting a price.

For common emergencies, our burst pipe repair and drain cleaning pages explain what the plumber will do once on site.

Local note

Southlake water bills, leaks and who to call

Southlake Public Works handles water and sewer, and its after-hours line is (817) 748-8082. The town's main line is 817-748-8400, and customer service is (817) 748-8051. A leak in the street is a call for Public Works first.

The town offers a leak adjustment on residential water bills once in a rolling twelve-month period. You need a registered Eye on Water account, you must document the repair within 90 days and pool fills are not eligible. EyeOnWater is a free app that sends leak alerts. A plumber's invoice is the usual proof, so keep it.

For permits on larger work, Southlake Building Inspections is at 1400 Main Street, Suite 250, phone (817) 748-8236.

Source type: Town of Southlake published pages for Public Works, leak adjustments and Building Inspections.

About Southlake

Southlake was incorporated in 1956. Census counts show the population at 7,065 in 1990, 21,519 in 2000 and 31,265 in 2020, so most of the town grew in a few decades. Southlake Town Square, at State Highway 114 and Southlake Boulevard, is its best-known landmark.

Plumbing in a house depends on its own age and pipe material, not the town around it. According to the EPA, the average family wastes about 9,400 gallons of water a year to leaks. A plumber will tell you what is in your walls when they see it, and a leak detection visit or a camera sewer inspection shows the real condition of your lines.

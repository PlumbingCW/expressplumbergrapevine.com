Plumbing in Glade Crossing, southeast Grapevine

Glade Crossing is a residential neighborhood in southeast Grapevine. Community Impact describes it as lying between State Highway 121 and State Highway 360, north of Glade Road. The paper reported about 571 homes in a 2015 profile, with build-out in 1993. A 2018 profile gave about 550 homes across four neighborhoods and build-out in 1994, so the counts differ a little by source.

The homes feed into Grapevine-Colleyville ISD, and the neighborhood has a public park on Westbury Drive. A neighborhood this size built in the early 1990s is now roughly three decades old, and that age is the angle for this page.

What a home that age usually needs

Houses finished in 1993 or 1994 are old enough that original equipment has often been replaced at least once. That does not mean yours has problems. It means some parts are worth checking, such as the water heater, the shutoff valves and the faucets.

The 2018 profile put home sizes at 1,350 to 3,650 square feet, which covers everything from small family homes to larger ones. Larger homes with several bathrooms have more fixtures that can leak or wear out.

What to do if a leak or backup starts

A few quick steps limit damage before anyone arrives.

Shut off the water

Turn off the main valve, or the fixture's stop valve if the leak is at one sink or toilet.

Stop using the drains

If a drain is backing up, do not flush or run water until it is cleared.

Check the water heater

If it is leaking, turn off its cold water supply and the power or gas.

Take photos

Pictures of damage and the source help with any insurance claim.

Local note

Grapevine water and hard water scale

Grapevine's 2024 water quality report lists 117 ppm hardness for the city's own treated water and 135 ppm for water bought from the Trinity River Authority. That is hard enough to leave scale on fixtures and inside water heaters over the years.

For permits, the City of Grapevine publishes a combined plumbing permit application that includes a residential water heater change-out. Ask Building Services at (817) 410-3165 which permit your job needs.

Source type: City of Grapevine 2024 water quality report, and the City of Grapevine permit application.

Questions about plumbing in Glade Crossing

Where is Glade Crossing in Grapevine?

Community Impact places it between State Highway 121 and State Highway 360, north of Glade Road, in southeast Grapevine. It has a public park on Westbury Drive.

How old are the homes in Glade Crossing?

Community Impact reported build-out in 1993 in a 2015 profile and in 1994 in a 2018 profile, so most homes are about thirty years old. Individual homes may have been updated since, and a plumber can read the date on your water heater.

Should I replace a water heater in a home this age?

It depends on the unit, not the neighborhood. If the tank is leaking, rusted at the base or no longer heats well, a replacement is likely. See our water heater replacement page for what the job involves.

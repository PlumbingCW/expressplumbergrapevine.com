Plumbing in Silver Lake Estates, northeast Grapevine

Silver Lake Estates sits in northeastern Grapevine, off Dove Loop Road between Main Street and Dooley Street. Community Impact describes the Villas and Manors sections together as about 350 single-family homes, built out in 2007 by David Weekley Homes. The paper gave home sizes of 2,000 to 4,750 square feet.

It is a newer community than most of Grapevine, and it is within walking distance of Main Street according to the same profile. That makes this page about larger, newer homes, not older ones.

Newer homes still need plumbing attention

A house built out around 2007 is close to twenty years old. That is young for a house but old for some parts. Water heaters, faucets, toilet fill valves and garbage disposals are the usual wear items, and they are worth checking on any home of this age.

Larger homes have more bathrooms and longer pipe runs, so a leak can go unnoticed for longer. A water meter check is a simple first step if the bill climbs for no clear reason.

What to do when you find a leak

In a larger house, a leak can run a while before it shows. Act quickly once you notice one.

Close the main valve

Know where the main shutoff is and make sure everyone in the house does too.

Check the meter

Turn off all water and look at the meter. If it still moves, water is going somewhere.

Open a low faucet

Draining the lines can relieve pressure while you wait.

Photograph and dry

Document the damage, then move items out of the wet area.

Local note

Permits and hard water in Grapevine

Grapevine's 2024 water quality report lists 117 ppm hardness for the city's own treated water and 135 ppm for water bought from the Trinity River Authority. Hard water is a common reason homeowners ask about softeners and about descaling tankless units.

The City of Grapevine publishes a combined plumbing permit application that includes a residential water heater change-out. Building Services at (817) 410-3165 can say which permit applies.

Source type: City of Grapevine 2024 water quality report, and the City of Grapevine permit application.

Emergency plumber

An emergency plumber in Grapevine, TX who answers at 2 a.m.

A plumbing emergency in Grapevine usually shows up at the worst time: a supply line splits under the kitchen sink on a Saturday night, a main line backs up into the tub, or a water heater starts leaking into the garage before sunrise. Grapevine Express Plumbing runs a 24/7 emergency line for exactly those calls, and the number is (817) 601-6266.

Start with the water. If a pipe has burst, find the main shutoff valve and turn it clockwise until it stops, then open the lowest faucet in the house to drain what is left. That one step stops most of the damage while a plumber is on the way. Our guide to burst pipe repair walks through what happens after the water is off.

If drains are the problem, stop using every fixture that sends water down the line. A slow tub that gurgles when the washer drains usually means the blockage is in the main line, and more water only pushes it back into the lowest drain in the house. A plumber can clear it with drain cleaning, and if the same line keeps backing up, a camera shows why.

No hot water is also an emergency in January. Most tank failures come down to a burned-out element, a failed thermostat, a pilot or igniter problem, or a tank that has finally rusted through, and each has a different fix. Water heater repair comes first when the tank is sound. When it is leaking from the shell, water heater replacement is the honest answer.

Do this first

  1. Shut off the main water valve if water is running.
  2. Open the lowest faucet to relieve pressure.
  3. Stop using drains if sewage is backing up.
  4. Turn off power to wet areas if it is safe to reach the panel.
  5. Call (817) 601-6266 and tell the dispatcher what you did.

Grapevine specifics

What is different about plumbing in Grapevine

Every city has its own water, its own code and its own history of bad weeks. These are the Grapevine facts that change how we approach a call, each taken from the city's own published documents.

Water with minerals in it

Grapevine's 2024 water quality report lists 117 ppm hardness for the city's own treated water and 135 ppm for water bought from the Trinity River Authority. That is scale-forming water, and it is why tankless units need descaling. See water softener and filtration for what to do about it.

A code that changed in 2024

The city lists the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), effective January 1, 2024, with its own amendments. For a water heater installation or a repipe, ask Building Services at (817) 410-3165 which permit applies.

February 2021 showed the limit

During Winter Storm Uri the city used about 20 million gallons on February 16, against a normal winter day near 5 million. The city also waived plumbing repair permit fees afterward. Our burst pipe repair page covers what to do before the next freeze.

A visitor economy runs on hot water

Main Street restaurants, the resorts on Lake Grapevine and the retail around Grapevine Mills depend on kitchens, restrooms and water heating through peak weekends. Commercial plumbing here is mostly about preventing a Friday failure.

Emergency plumbing questions in Grapevine

Is there an emergency plumber in Grapevine open at night and on weekends?

Yes. The emergency line at (817) 601-6266 is open 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Describe what is happening and a dispatcher books the soonest appointment available. Same-day appointments are offered when the schedule allows.

What should I do before the plumber arrives?

For a burst pipe or active leak, shut off the main water valve and open a faucet at the lowest point of the house to relieve pressure. For a sewage backup, stop running water down every drain. For a water heater leak, turn off the heat source and close the cold water valve above the tank.

Keep people and pets out of any room with standing water near outlets, and move valuables up off the floor.

How do I know what the job will cost?

The plumber on site finds the cause first and gives you a clear price before work starts. You approve it or you decline it. Prices depend on the cause, the access, the parts and the time of day, so a number quoted over the phone for a problem nobody has seen yet is only a guess.

Do you cover Southlake and other towns near Grapevine?

Yes. Grapevine is the base, and Southlake has its own page because it sits right across Lake Grapevine's north shore. We also take calls from Colleyville, Coppell, Euless and Flower Mound, so call and give the address.

Do I need a permit for a water heater or a repipe in Grapevine?

The City of Grapevine publishes an electrical, plumbing, mechanical and irrigation permit application that includes a residential water heater change-out category. Permit rules differ by job, so ask Building Services at (817) 410-3165 about your specific work before it starts.

Are the plumbers licensed?

Yes, we send licensed and insured plumbers. You can check any plumber's license yourself on the Public License Search of the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners at tsbpe.texas.gov, and you should for any contractor you hire.
